Hub Art Picker

Three options each for the player, the cabinets, the room and the prize counter (issue #17, PR #43), all in Sweetie 16. Pick one per row; the hub on the left redraws at the game's own layout. The player walks itself round until you take over.

Arrow keys or WASD walk the player. Leave it for three seconds and it walks itself again.

Positions match the real hub: cabinets on tiles 6, 9 and 12 of row 5, the prize counter at 60,106, the player's 16×24 box blocked by the walls and the counter. Prompts and prize decorations are left out.
