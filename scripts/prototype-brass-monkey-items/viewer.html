Brass Monkey items

The things the Gnome sends down and the thing the monkey collects: the gear (a hazard that rolls down the girders), the ember (a hazard that wanders the girders and climbs ladders) and the bolt (worth points). None of them exist yet, so every option is new. Click a card to pick one of each; the strip at the bottom plays your picks with the monkey and the Gnome at 2×. Then press Copy my picks and paste the line back, with any notes ("G3, but brass").
